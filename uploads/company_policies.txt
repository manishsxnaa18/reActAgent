ACME Corporation - Company Policy Manual
Last Updated: January 2026

1. REMOTE WORK POLICY

Effective January 2025, ACME Corporation has adopted a hybrid work model:
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Tuesday and Thursday are designated in-office days
- Managers may approve exceptions based on business needs
- Remote work equipment is provided by the company
- Employees must maintain a dedicated workspace at home

2. VACATION POLICY

All full-time employees are entitled to:
- Year 1: 15 days paid vacation
- Year 2-5: 20 days paid vacation  
- Year 5+: 25 days paid vacation
- Vacation days must be approved by direct manager
- Unused vacation days roll over up to 5 days maximum

3. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Employees may be reimbursed for business expenses including:
- Travel (flights, hotels, ground transportation)
- Client meals (up to $75 per person)
- Office supplies and equipment (pre-approved)
- Professional development courses (up to $2,000 per year)

Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense.
Reimbursement will be processed within 2 weeks.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT

All employees must:
- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Protect confidential company information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report any unethical behavior to HR
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

5. HEALTH AND SAFETY

ACME is committed to providing a safe workplace:
- Regular safety training is mandatory
- Report all incidents immediately
- Emergency exits clearly marked
- First aid kits available on every floor
- Mental health support available through EAP

For questions about these policies, contact HR at hr@acmecorp.com
